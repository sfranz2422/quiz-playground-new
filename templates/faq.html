How Does Quiz Playground Work?

Quiz Playground aims to provide fun and interactive formative assessment through games. In our platform, the students are able to play any game on the site, leaving the teacher to focus on the student data.

All of our games are set up to display any of our quizzes, or you can use your own. The students can choose any game then simply enter the Quiz ID and Teacher ID to start the game. That's it! All the quiz results will be sent to the teacher dashboard in the results section.

Yes! Students can play any game on the site for free, and there is never any registration required for students. The students simply enter the Quiz ID to begin the game. Students can also click the trivia button to play a game with trivia questions instead of teacher quizzes.
If the teacher has a premium acccount the students can also enter the teacher ID at the beginning of the game so assessment results are reported to the teacher.

Also, with premium, teachers can copy and edit any of the public quizzes posted by other teachers.
